Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 August 2026

Who we are

English Online Forum ("we", "us", "our") is an independent, 100% online English school. This policy explains what information we collect when you use this website, our free placement test, or contact us, and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Contact details you give us — your name and a phone number, WhatsApp number, or email address, when you fill in the placement test form, the contact form, or message us directly.
  • Placement test answers and results — the CEFR level and section scores produced when you take a placement or practice test, so we can recommend the right class.
  • Materials access codes — if you are an enrolled student, the access code you use to view your level's learning materials.

We do not ask for payment card details, passwords, or government ID through this website.

How we use it

  • To recommend and place you in the right class or level.
  • To reply to your inquiry, by WhatsApp, phone, or email — whichever you shared with us.
  • To keep our course materials access limited to enrolled students.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

Placement test results

When you finish a placement or practice test, your result is shown to you on-screen. It is only sent to our team if you choose to tap "Send my result to English Online Forum," which opens WhatsApp with your result pre-filled for you to send. We do not automatically capture or store your answers on our servers.

Cookies and third-party services

This site loads fonts from Google Fonts and icon assets from a content delivery network, which may receive your browser's IP address as part of loading those files, in line with their own privacy policies. We do not currently use analytics or advertising cookies on this site.

How long we keep information

We keep contact details and enrollment information for as long as you are an active or prospective student, or as needed to respond to your inquiry, after which we delete it on request.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time to tell you what information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it, by contacting us using the details below.
